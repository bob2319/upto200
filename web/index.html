      How it works

      Upto200 is a free online image optimizer built for web performance: smaller assets, clearer controls, and honest results when a target cannot be reached.

      1. 1

        Upload

        Add JPG, PNG, WebP, or HEIC files — one image or a full batch. Invalid files are reported without stopping the rest of the queue.

      2. 2

        Choose target & format

        Set a KB budget (for example 100, 150, or 200), limit max width, pick WebP/JPEG/PNG, and adjust quality bounds. Already-small images may not shrink further.

      3. 3

        Preview & download

        Compare original and optimized previews, check reduction stats, then download files individually or as a ZIP archive.
